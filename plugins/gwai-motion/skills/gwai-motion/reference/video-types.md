# Video types

Offer these in the interview, named after the real thing ("Beauty Decoded hackathon", "September cohort", "100 day challenge"), and let her pick or say "you choose". Each has a recommended style ([styles.md](styles.md)); she can switch.

Bars assume 140 BPM for a showreel (a bar is 1.71 s) and 88 BPM for soft and lesson (a bar is 2.73 s). Every film ends on the lockup, held 2 s or more, landing on a downbeat so a Reel loops cleanly.

| Type | Style | Length | Chapters | Ending | What she needs to give |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Event or hackathon** | Showreel | 15 to 20 s | the hook (the challenge in 2 words) → what (the name slams) → when and where (the date and city slam, a pill each) → who it is for, with the reassurance line in capitals → the prize or the perk, if there is one | lockup + "APPLY NOW" or "LINK IN BIO" | name, date, time, place (or online), price or free, seats, prizes, partners, the link |
| **Cohort or course launch** | Showreel or soft | 18 to 25 s | her real week (the pain as a picture) → the promise as a paired fragment → the roadmap: 3 numbered cards, one per week or module → the room (seats, format) | lockup + "APPLY NOW", the start date | name, dates, length, format, price, seats, the 3 or 4 things women walk out with |
| **AI tip or tool tutorial** | Lesson | 20 to 45 s | the hook in her words ("Still writing every email yourself?") → steps 1 to 3, each with the prompt box or a screenshot → the takeaway card | lockup + "SAVE THIS" or "FOLLOW FOR MORE" | the tool, the exact prompt(s), the real answer or a screenshot of it |
| **Challenge** (100 days, a build a week) | Showreel | 15 to 20 s | the number slams ("100 DAYS.") → what she builds ("100 BUILDS.") → how it works, 3 badges → low pressure line ("Go at your own pace.") | lockup + the join CTA | name, length, start date, how to join, what it costs |
| **Event recap** | Soft | 15 to 25 s | the room (her photos, one at a time as cards) → the number ("40+ women. Two hours.") → what they built (screenshots of real builds) → thank you + what comes next | lockup + the next event, if there is one | photos (with consent), the headcount, what was built, the next date |
| **Member spotlight** | Soft | 15 to 20 s | her photo and name (with consent) → what she built, in one line → her words, as a quote card → what she used (tool names) | lockup | the member's photo, name, quote (approved by her), the build |
| **Send this to her** (recruitment) | Soft | 10 to 15 s | "You know that friend who keeps saying "I have an idea"?" → what GWAI is in 3 statements → "Send this to her." | lockup + "TAG HER" | the offer or event it points to |
| **Deadline or last call** | Showreel or soft | 8 to 12 s | the date slams → what closes → how many places are left (only if she gave a number) | lockup + "APPLY NOW" | the real deadline and the real number |
| **Quote or stat** | Soft | 8 to 12 s | the line, word by word on cream → who said it, or the source of the number | lockup | the exact quote or figure and its source |

## Rules for every type

- **Real facts only.** Dates, prices, seats, prizes, partners, headcounts and quotes come from her, word for word. A missing fact is left out, never guessed.
- **One message.** The viewer repeats it after one watch, in three short statements at most: "Two hours. One room. One working product."
- **Numbers are the hero.** When she gives a number (50 places, 100 days, 40+ women), it gets its own beat, its own slam or its own card.
- **Ending by type.** Events and launches end on the CTA and the date; lessons on "SAVE THIS"; recaps on the next thing; spotlights and quotes on the lockup alone.
- **Series.** A weekly tip, a challenge day by day, a cohort week by week: same style, same chapter labels, same music key, same ending. Change the content only.
