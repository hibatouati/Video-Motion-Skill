# Interview: ask little, remember everything

The brand is known ([gwai-brand.md](gwai-brand.md)). Never ask about colours, fonts, the logo, the voice or the audience. Ask only what this film needs.

Always ask with the **AskUserQuestion** tool (never as a list in chat): at most 4 questions per call, 2 to 4 options each ("Other" is added automatically for free text), options named after what she said or sent, `multiSelect` where several answers fit. **Every question has a "You choose for me" option**; when there is a clear best answer, put it first and mark it "(Recommended)". If the tool is unavailable, ask the same questions in one short numbered message with the defaults stated.

## Step 0: the profile and what she sent

Read `videos/BRAND.md` first: standing choices (format, style per type, music, handle, CTA wording) and notes on past films. Reuse all of it and say in one line what you reused ("Using your saved choices: 9:16, lo-fi for lessons, ends on LINK IN BIO").

Open every file she sent. A photo of an event says "recap"; a screenshot of a chat says "lesson"; a poster with a date says "event". Use that to pre-select the type.

## Round 1: the film (one call)

| Question | Options (adapt to what you found) |
|---|---|
| What kind of video? | The types from [video-types.md](video-types.md) that fit what she sent, with the best one "(Recommended)": Event or hackathon / Cohort launch / AI tip or tutorial / Challenge / Event recap / Member spotlight / Send this to her / Deadline / Quote or stat / You choose for me |
| Which style? | The style that fits the type "(Recommended)", then the other two: Showreel (fast, 3D, trap beat) / Soft (calm, cream, lo-fi) / Lesson (step by step, prompt box) / You choose for me |
| Where will it play, and how long? | Reel / TikTok, 9:16, 15 to 20 s (Recommended) / Story, 9:16, under 15 s / Square post, 1:1, 15 s / LinkedIn, 16:9 or 1:1, 20 to 30 s / You choose for me |
| What music? | The style's default "(Recommended)": a generated trap beat or a generated lo-fi bed, royalty-free / My own licensed track / Silent with captions (reads muted) / You choose for me |

## Round 2: the facts (only what the type needs)

Ask for the exact facts the type needs and she has not given: the date and time, the place or "online", the price or "free", the number of places, prizes, partners, the link or "link in bio", the prompt and the real answer for a lesson. Ask in one call, one question per missing group, each with "Leave it out" as an option. Never invent a fact to fill a gap.

For the one message, offer 2 or 3 candidates written in the GWAI voice from what she gave, each as three short statements, plus "You choose for me".

If photos show people, ask once: "Do the women in these photos know they will be in a public video?" with "Yes, they agreed" / "Blur or skip faces" / "Only use photos without faces".

## Remember for next time

Write the answers down as soon as you have them:
- **`videos/BRAND.md`**: standing choices (format, style per type, music, handle, CTA wording) and "Chosen by Claude" for anything picked with "You choose for me".
- **`videos/<film>/PROMPT.md`**: this film's type, style, message, facts, assets and one-off choices.
- Add the film to "Films" in `BRAND.md` with her reaction. Notes on one film ("less text", "slower", "more photos") become standing choices.

## When she is away or says "just go"

Use the profile, else the recommended options, write them under "Assumed, not asked" in `BRAND.md`, tell her in one line, and continue. Leave out any fact you do not have and list what you left out. Show 3 style frames as soon as they exist.

## When she shows a reference video

Study it before building: a contact sheet every 0.25 s, then name what makes it work (pace, type scale, how the picture changes, transitions, the ending). Apply those to GWAI; never copy the reference's colours, words or marks.

## Don't ask

- Colours, fonts, logo, voice, audience: the brand answers them.
- What the profile already answers.
- Taste you can show instead: make 3 style frames and let her react.
